About the Firm

A practice rooted in relationships, not billable hours.

For over thirty years, Elliot J. Fishman P.C. has provided exceptional legal counsel at affordable prices. We represent individuals, lenders, transportation companies, and estates across New York and New Jersey.

Our work is methodical, our communication direct, and our fee structure transparent. Most of our clients return — and refer their families.
